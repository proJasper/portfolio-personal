Welcome to my blog! The idea is to use this space as a journal; a place where I document things I'm working on, challenges I run into, and how I approach solving them. I've learned the hard way that publicly promising to post regularly is usually the fastest way to do the opposite, so I won't.

From time to time I'll also try to share more structured posts, like small tutorials or technical deep dives. Nothing formal, just practical things that worked for me.

That's it for now. Let's see where this goes.
