## My First Post

Hey 👋

I’ve learned the hard way that publicly promising to post regularly is usually the fastest way to do the opposite, so I won’t. This space is meant to be more of a journal. A place where I document things I’m working on, challenges I run into, and how I approach solving them.

From time to time, I’ll also share more structured posts, like small tutorials or technical deep dives. Nothing too formal, just practical things that worked for me.

That’s it for now. Let’s see where this goes.
